# Cooperative stagewise learning: implementation contract

This contract describes the executable static, two-agent implementation, including the opt-in surveyed-maze extension. It does not claim that all mechanisms proposed in the [research formulation](../docs/COOPERATIVE_STAGEWISE_PH_FORMULATION.md) are implemented or beneficial. Status labels distinguish **implemented**, **approximated**, **experimental**, and **deferred**. Existing benchmark results and the original formulation remain unchanged.

| Formulation mechanism | Current executable behavior and status | Source and checks |
|---|---|---|
| Ledger; current plus $B\in\{2,3\}$ earlier stages | **Implemented.** Bounded `Stage` records contain immutable readings/transitions, entries, anchors, versions, curvature, footprints, and counterfactuals. Default $B=2$; $B=0,3$ are ablations. Original stage movement is along x; the maze uses horizontal/vertical movement and backtracking, with 30% nominal rectangular overlap. Entries are measured states, not synthetic observations. | `records.py`, `learner.py`, `maze_learning.py`; `test_core.py`, `test_stage_prediction.py`, `test_maze.py` |
| pH equations (1)–(4), learning-energy separation (6) | **Implemented specialization.** Unit mass and known input map; positive scalar damping is identified. `flow` integrates a held force exactly. `balance` accounts for exact actuator work, dissipated heat, and kinetic change. Physical stored energy is $K=\|p\|^2/2$. Exported/displayed shaping Hamiltonian is $\widehat H=K+V$ under an explicit convention. Learning, geometry, and target jumps are not actuator work. | `model.py`, `cooperative.py`, `navigation.py`; `test_core.py`, `test_navigation.py`, `test_progress.py` |
| Environmental representation (5) | **Implemented specialization.** A 4×4 Gaussian RBF dictionary per stage, $\widehat r(q)=\operatorname{softplus}(\phi(q)^Ta)/4$. Fits use inverse-softplus of $4r$, ridge regularization centered at previous coefficients, and reserved readings. This is not a general neural Hamiltonian. | `model.py`, `learner.py::_field_fit`; `test_core.py` |
| Forward identification, A2 / (10)–(13) | **Approximated objective.** Executed-input state residual plus a weighted trapezoidal measured-energy residual. Scalar Newton-like damping steps with numerical bounds, followed by trust/bound reconciliation. Mass and input gain are fixed. The exact physical work ledger differs intentionally from the finite-step fitting residual. | `learner.py::_plant_loss`, `_fit_damping`; `test_core.py` |
| Overlap consistency (14) | **Implemented value-only approximation.** Retained fields are fit jointly. Weighted rows match adjacent latent RBF values at anchors supported by both stages; monotonic softplus then aligns values. No gradient matching, learned offset, or general gauge alignment. | `learner.py::_field_fit`, `records.py`; `test_core.py::test_overlap_changes_earlier_model_and_policy_gradient` |
| Historical reconstruction and replay, A3 / §5.2 | **Implemented static approximation.** Frozen reconstructed plant, environmental fields, sensed geometry, and route context; differentiable policy rollouts from retained entries. Retained-domain replay can hand off between known stages; unsupported motion or unavailable successors invalidate a batch. These are explicitly static-current-environment counterfactuals, not inference of historical environmental truth. | `stage_prediction.py`, `replay_budget.py`; `test_stage_prediction.py`, `test_receiver_feedback.py`, `test_maze.py` |
| Replay horizon and compute | **Implemented.** Fixed eight-step default; opt-in supported-boundary pilot seeks a known handoff plus four steps, capped at 32. Frozen per-update horizons and starts; 1,024-flow ceiling includes guard evaluations and diagnostic guards. No-history/current-state comparisons retain their declared evidence budgets. Attribution's current-state arm uses one current start, not the older repeated-current-state padding comparator. | `replay_budget.py`, `stage_prediction.py`, `diagnostics.py`; `test_receiver_feedback.py`, `test_attribution.py` |
| Error proposal and physical reconciliation, A4 / §5.4 | **Implemented restricted solver, not general constrained inference.** Two policy scalars; one gradient proposal, box/trust projection and bounded backtracking against frozen replay. Damping and fields are fit separately. Every fifth local sample/transition is reserved; measured validation and feasibility gate commitment. Invalid policy proposals roll back. Unsupported replay prevents policy optimization but does not automatically reject an otherwise valid measured-evidence model update. | `learner.py::update`; `test_core.py`, `test_progress.py` |
| Packet (17)–(18) | **Implemented.** Versioned $q,p,v,\widehat H,V^{task}$, goal/convention, environmental value/gradient anchors, covariance, support, age and source identities. Sender export reconstructs from local measured readings only. Maze extends the packet with independently provenance-bearing sensed geometry and footprints. A packet is a supported summary, not the entire receiver/world model. | `cooperative.py::Packet`, `maze_learning.py::MazePacket`; `test_cooperative.py`, `test_maze.py` |
| Receiver update (19)–(21) | **Approximated.** Identity, convention, age, provenance, sequence, covariance and support checks; separate received stores; replacement of sender contribution. One Gauss–Newton field step with local curvature, componentwise step clamp, and covariance addition $0.01+0.005\delta$. This is not covariance intersection or a calibrated posterior. Existing fit eligibility uses retained stage rectangle containment; it does not certify new local replay support. | `cooperative.py::assimilate`, `_field_fit`; `test_cooperative.py`, `test_attribution.py` |
| Uncertainty, information gain and rate (22)–(26) | **Experimental.** Ridge curvature is not posterior uncertainty. Local block-bootstrap packet covariance (default eight members), optional calibration scale, overlap-discounted log-det score, and bounded rate objective exist. Recipient-value prediction is a limited-horizon fitted model; later compact-summary and exact-receiver diagnostics expose validity/acceptance limitations. Attribution uses fixed-rate exchange only. | `cooperative.py`, `scheduling.py`, `stagewise_diagnostics.py`, `packet_feedback.py`; associated cooperative/feedback tests and preserved reports |
| Scalar shaping and hard safety | **Implemented extension.** Progress shaping differentiates the complete tapered potential and freezes the conservative bound from model context. Nominal control includes damping feedback. Actuator/speed/barrier projection plus swept/stopping guards constrain it. Scalar potential radial progress does not guarantee projected-controller completion in arbitrary unknown environments. | `model.py`, `navigation.py`; `test_progress.py`, `test_navigation.py` |
| Maze routing prior | **Implemented experimental extension.** Public surveyed corridor graph and footprint are shared equally. Hidden gates are sensed or reported. Graph routing removes edges intersecting known closures; local/remote anchor Taylor fields supply route costs with fixed unknown-risk value 1. It is not unknown-map exploration and is not the general observed-space A* controller. | `maze_geometry.py`, `maze_learning.py::maze_route`, `terrain_warning.py::terrain_values`; `test_maze.py`, `test_terrain_warning.py` |
| Remote route scoring versus learning | **Distinct implemented paths.** Anchors may be accepted for remote route scoring outside retained reconstruction support. They do not become local readings, identify damping, or certify replay support. Geometry can revise target and protection even when the learned parameter update is rejected. A detour alone therefore does not prove field assimilation or policy replay helped. | `terrain_warning.py::assimilate`, `maze_learning.py::assimilate`; `test_attribution.py` |

| Across-episode learning (§9.3) | **Deferred.** Every episode initializes fresh learners. All reported learning is within-mission adaptation. | Experiment factories and frozen manifests |

## Attribution experiment contract

**Route-representation clarification:** local route anchors are evaluations of the reconstructed stage fields, not a separate field-independent path. The original anchor envelope already transfers reconstruction changes into route scoring indirectly. The opt-in `route_field_mode="supported_field"` experiment evaluates committed RBFs directly on retained local support, with legacy anchor/unknown fallback elsewhere. See [direct-field routing](FIELD_ROUTE_RESULTS.md) for its comparison and snapshot contract.

`python -m coph_stagewise.attribution_suite suite --out results/attribution_v1 --profile all --workers 4` freezes both the 28-episode smoke and 112-episode development specifications before execution. Development reuses smoke episodes only under identical source/configuration and artifact fingerprints. Existing outputs are never overwritten by a different configuration. `report` and `render` subcommands regenerate analysis and visualization from saved records.

The seven arms separate local evidence, local historical replay, shared geometry, remote route anchors, supported field assimilation, current-state replay, and retained-entry replay. All communication arms send the unchanged full packet on the existing fixed opportunity schedule and charge unused payload components. Disabled receiver paths do not bypass payload validation. Local fitting, validation, reconciliation and rollback remain active in every arm.

Blocked maze geometry is unchanged. Soft-risk maze removes gates and places traversable Gaussian risks at their centers with widths `(0.5,0.38)` and amplitude `4`. Both use 1,500 active steps per agent, starts at 0/5 seconds, default safety/controller parameters, four million bytes per agent, and packet lifetime 300 seconds. Seeds 75000–75007 change indexed sensor noise; they are not eight different maps. Seed 75000 is selected for both cooperative animations before execution. All experiments are development-only.

First-valid-packet audits use private cloned receivers and true-world continuations at 8/16/32 steps. Actual retained measurements validate the initial branch update. Subsequent simulated observations remain in isolated hypothetical branches. No audit values enter operational fitting, routing, scheduling, or transmitted packets. Immediate effects use identical physical state, and ordered field/route/policy decomposition is not a unique additive causal explanation. Missing support and zero effects remain reported.

Navigation readiness, demonstrated packet-to-model/control changes, and additional benefit from reconstruction or historical replay are separate claims. Completion does not imply all three; lower mission cost is not a readiness requirement.
