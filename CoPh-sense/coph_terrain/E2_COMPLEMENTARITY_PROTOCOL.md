# Separate E2-Complementarity challenge protocol

This document fixes the challenge selection rule before any CoPH model is
scored on it. It is a **controlled mechanism test**, not an estimate of how
often complementary sensing is useful in E2-Natural. Its procedural generator
and manifest are not yet implemented; no challenge result is claimed.

Use the frozen E2-Natural sensor ranges/footprints, sensing and packet costs,
deadline, resource caps, pH executor, and material-risk formula. A separate
challenge generator may create early route alternatives and latent surface/
traction dependencies; those modifications must be documented in its own
source hash. Public task geometry and candidate rights must be identical
across all compared methods. The evaluator alone may inspect the complete
latent world or counterfactual returns when admitting a challenge case.

For the acquisition stratum, identify a physically reachable early target
with legal geometry and contact-traction measurements. At a common agent-
visible information state, evaluate skip, each singleton, and the pair as
**expected** physical cost over the declared latent prior. Couple packet and
appearance randomness across actions and charge physical travel/dwell. A
single realized world's hindsight cost is not an acquisition oracle. Admit the case
only when every branch completes, both pair readings arrive while actionable,
and

\[
Q(G,T)+0.02 < \min\{Q(G),Q(T),Q(\varnothing)\}.
\]

Keep separate redundant and additive control strata, defined by these same
physical branch values, and report the source-pool acceptance rate for each.
Never screen or resample by a learned CoPH decision or return.

The first acquisition-stratum prototype uses two public material rectangles.
Region 2 is declared safe and known to both agents in that stratum, so the
four equiprobable latent states of Region 1 are the entire hidden-state
ensemble at admission. A separate memory stratum will leave Region 2 unknown;
its conditional labels must marginalize over that additional latent state.
For the restricted physical continuation, the carrier may wait at a public
staging position when the team selects scout sensing, until the selected
readings are delivered. Waiting time is charged in full. The skip branch
proceeds immediately. This is a specified cooperative continuation policy,
not a claim of globally optimal decentralized control.

For the memory stratum, clone a post-delivery physical state and intervene
only on the recipient's legitimately delivered evidence. Require a pre-
declared actionable pair of later acquisition choices, all physical branches
successful, and a margin greater than 0.02 such that the restricted best
choice changes from sensing unresolved Region 1 without the message to
skipping or sensing Region 2 with it. Record the exact recipient-visible
history and the full team cost, including the scout's sensing and packet.

Create disjoint challenge train, validation, and test parent groups under
separate seeds, and hash generator, admission code, accepted group list, and
all physical configuration sources before confirmatory fitting. Hold the
test manifest unread by tuning code. Report challenge regret and complete-
policy cost separately from Natural cost/CVaR; never pool their frequencies.
